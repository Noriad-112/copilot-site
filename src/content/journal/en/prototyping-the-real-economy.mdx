Theories are useful, but the real economy is shaped by daily decisions, logistics, and relationships. We prototype in the places where life happens: kitchens, fields, community rooms, and governance circles.

Each experiment is an opportunity to test a different pattern of ownership, care, or exchange. We document what works, what fails, and what needs another season to mature.

Over time, these prototypes become a shared library for regenerative practice.
