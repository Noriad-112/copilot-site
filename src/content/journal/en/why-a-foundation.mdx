title: "Why a foundation?"
slug: "why-a-foundation"
date: "2025-11-12"
summary: "A foundation can hold long-term intent while operators focus on day-to-day reality."
tags: ["governance", "stewardship"]
---

A foundation is a container for continuity. It can hold a mission that is bigger than a single project, protect the long view, and reduce pressure to optimize for quick returns.

For Roots & Remedies, the stichting is the center that keeps experiments aligned with regenerative intent. Operators can run real projects with autonomy, while the foundation keeps the mission coherent across time.

This allows us to prototype alternatives without losing focus on care, place, and durability.
